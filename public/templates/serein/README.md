# Serein — editorial stationery

Stable key: `serein`. Ivory paper / plum ink; Crimson Pro + DM Sans; photo theme with cover, personOne, personTwo and gallery roles. The owner delegated art direction on 30 September 2026. Section-specific generated references were inspected before continuing implementation.

`botanical-engraving.webp` is a transparent, optimized derivative of the original illustration generated for this theme. It is used as a contain-sized alpha mask, tinted with invitation palette tokens; no customer data is baked into the artwork. Folded paper and wax seal are semantic DOM/CSS layers with independent Studio ownership. Music uses the existing local Until We Meet Again track and its existing project licensing.

The same universal renderer supplies all 15 controls, event-scoped photo assignments, copy, native objects, RSVP, wishes, gift, countdown, maps and music. The album opens a keyboard/swipe-enabled native dialog on public invitations; previews cannot submit data or open gallery dialogs while editing. Original generated references are preserved outside public; no licensed source artwork was moved or overwritten.

Validation and remaining browser/live-save checks are recorded in `prd.md` Appendix A.
